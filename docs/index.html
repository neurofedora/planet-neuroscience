    Network analysis investigating the differentiation of achievement goal orientations in junior high school [version 2; peer review: 2 approved]

    Achievement goal orientation theory has become popular, as it interprets students’ academic attitudes and behavior. The present research aims to investigate the variation in teachers’ perceived goals in relation to achievement goals in different grades, as well as the emergence of the multiple goal theory. Participants were junior high school students who responded to a self-report questionnaire. The instrument used was the Patterns of Adaptive Learning Scales (PALS). The results revealed that mastery and performance goals behave as a network of interacting variables that essentially represent their coexistence, as well as their individual variations and specificities in relation to other variables. The present research contributes to the theoretical development of the field, while in practice it highlights the combination of goals in school reality.

    Online Gambling Detection Systems: A Systematic Review [version 1; peer review: awaiting peer review]

    Background Illegal online gambling increasingly exploits websites, mobile applications, social media, messaging services, and decentralized financial infrastructure, and operators actively evade automated filtering. This review synthesizes how online gambling detection systems (OGDS) are applied, integrated, and evaluated, and which limitations constrain their deployment. Methods We searched IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect, and Scopus on 9 January 2026. Peer-reviewed English-language journal and conference studies in computer science published from 2021 to 2025 and reporting measurable evaluation metrics were eligible. We appraised methodological quality using a 10-item checklist and quantified inter-reviewer agreement with Cohen’s kappa. We synthesized evidence through frequency analysis and thematic synthesis across four research questions. Results Of 1,866 records identified, we included 39 studies (kappa = 0.83). Website detection dominated the corpus (15 studies), followed by harm detection (9), social media and spam messages (6), crypto and blockchain (4), mobile applications (3), and network infrastructure (2). Researchers identified six architectural integration patterns: multimodal feature fusion, graph-structural integration, hybrid ensemble learning, transformer-based semantic systems, harm systems, and rule-based operational systems. Multimodal fusion and graph-based models reported the highest detection scores against content obfuscation, whereas lightweight rule-based tools were favored for low-resource infrastructure. Evidence quality was uneven: 12 studies lacked a baseline comparison, 27 did not release data or code, and only six reported computational cost; differing datasets and protocols preclude direct performance comparison. Four recurrent limitations emerged: data quality and temporal drift; adversarial evasion and multimodal obfuscation; semantic ambiguity of localized slang and blockchain anonymity; and computational constraints. Conclusions Research priorities include adaptive recalibration, lightweight edge deployment, deep semantic entity extraction, explainable models for legal admissibility, and Web3 forensics. Shared, longitudinal, and cross-regional benchmarks are needed before performance claims can be generalized beyond individual study settings.

    Consumo de alimentos ultraprocesados, coexistencia de aditivos y factores de riesgo asociados entre estudiantes universitarios en Perú [version 3; peer review: 1 approved, 1 approved with reservations]

    Background The rising consumption of ultra-processed foods (UPFs) is a global concern, given their poor nutritional quality and the presence of multiple additives that may pose health risks when consumed in excess. Young populations are particularly vulnerable due to lifestyle habits and the widespread availability and accessibility of these products. The present study aimed to assess the consumption of ultra-processed foods (UPFs), taking into account the co-occurrence of additives and their association with risk factors in a young population. Methods A two-phase cross-sectional study was conducted. In the first phase, 500 ultra-processed food products available on the Peruvian market were analysed and classified into five groups: meat products, cereals, confectionery, dairy, and beverages, in order to identify the presence and frequency of additives. Conclusion It is concluded that excessive UPF consumption varies according to age and sex and represents a continuous source of exposure to food additives. The bibliometric analysis establishes the publication and thematic landscape, while the SLR extracts the theorized and conceptual framing from the academic literature.     Preoperative Eating Behaviour and Early Weight Loss as Predictors of 12-Month Success after Laparoscopic Sleeve Gastrectomy: A Single-Centre Study in Thai Patients [version 2; peer review: awaiting peer review]

    Objective This study examined the association between preoperative parameters and weight loss outcomes at 12 months after laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy (LSG). Materials and Methods We retrospectively reviewed 93 patients who underwent laparoscopic sleeve gastrectomy (LSG); the 68 with complete 12-month data formed the analytic cohort. Success was defined as a total weight loss (TWL) of at least 25% at 12 months. Eating behaviour was classified as “healthy” or “risky” using the validated Three-Factor Eating Questionnaire. Logistic regression, ROC analysis, and bootstrap internal validation were used to identify predictors and assess model performance. Results Of the 68 patients in the analytic cohort, 54.4% (n = 37) achieved successful weight loss. Risky eating behaviour was the only independent predictor of lower success (adjusted OR = 0.03, 95% CI: 0.01–0.14, p < 0.001); female sex was not significant after adjustment (OR = 0.40, p = 0.23). The final model (risky eating behaviour, baseline BMI, diabetes) showed an AUC of 0.89 (optimism-corrected 0.88), with sensitivity 86.5%, specificity 80.6%, and accuracy 83.8%. Conclusion Preoperative risky eating behaviour is a strong independent predictor of weight loss success after LSG, and early postoperative weight loss further identifies patients at risk of failure. Identifying patients with risky eating behaviour could help tailor preoperative counselling and postoperative support.

    arXiv:2610.29042v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Background: Gamma entrainment using sensory stimulation (GENUS) is under study as a treatment for neurological disease, but cortical engagement is variable.     A Reverse-Gold Theorem: Positive-Data Identification by Substrate-Bounded Contrastive Learners

    arXiv:2610.28747v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Gold's theorem shows that no learner can identify an arbitrary language in a superfinite class from positive presentations alone. We prove a positive result of a different shape. Fix an innate \emph{recursive contrast operator} $R$ mapping any string to a finite set of structurally bounded perturbations of it, and a discriminative scorer of bounded capacity trained by InfoNCE-style contrastive estimation \citep{vandenoord2018,macollins2018} on self-generated perturbations. Under a faithfulness condition relating operator, scorer, and target, the trained scorer achieves \emph{PAC conditional identification} from positive data alone: with high probability it ranks all but an $\varepsilon$-fraction of positives above every out-of-language perturbation in their own orbit, with sample complexity polynomial in the capacity, the orbit size, the score bound, and $1/\varepsilon$. The negatives are constructed by the learner, not supplied by an oracle, and no global normalizing constant over strings is ever computed. The misranking bound follows from the geometry of the contrastive loss---per-example loss floors plus a definite loss increment under misranking---yielding an $\varepsilon^{-2}$ rate, together with an explicit approximation gap that quantifies the cost of in-language (``colliding'') perturbations and vanishes for orbit-pure operators. The ranking guarantee lifts to a global membership classifier under a margin condition, and the same operator composed with the learned scorer is a normalization-free Metropolis--Hastings generator. We instantiate the hypotheses for substitutable context-free languages \citep{clarkeyraud2007} and the subregular classes \citep{heinz2010,hayashikamigaito2025}, and state an open conjecture locating the construction in the mildly context-sensitive hierarchy.

The votes' vector sum $c_1$ is the first circular moment of the doubled angles; half its angle, $\phi=\tfrac12\arg c_1$, is the induced vertical, a stimulus axis, not the judged upright. This is the orientation order-parameter model (PLUMB).     Distinct weak antisymmetric interactions shape human brain functions as probability fluxes

    arXiv:2508.20961v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: The functional computation of the human brain is hypothesized to arise from the collective behaviour of the underlying neural network.
