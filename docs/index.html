    Federal Authorization of Flavored ENDS Products has Greater Potential to Benefit Public Health than Complete Prohibition: A Population Health Simulation Study [version 1; peer review: awaiting peer review]

    Background The decline in cigarette smoking in the United States has coincided with increased use of electronic nicotine delivery systems (ENDS), particularly flavored products. However, numerous jurisdictions have prohibited flavored tobacco products, including menthol cigarettes and non-tobacco-flavored ENDS, raising concerns about unintended public health consequences including illicit markets or reversion to smoking. Methods We paired the Smoking and Vaping Model (SAVM) with recent Population Assessment of Tobacco and Health (PATH) Wave 6–7 data (2021–2023) to simulate the 36-year impact (2024–2060) of two federal regulatory scenarios: (1) Complete Authorization of flavored tobacco products and (2) Complete Prohibition of all flavored products, allowing only tobacco-flavored cigarettes and ENDS. Results Approximately 90% of ENDS use involved nontobacco flavors, with switching rates projected to be 2.3x higher under Complete Authorization. Although Complete Prohibition was estimated to double smoking cessation immediately after enactment, this impact was assumed to decline 10% annually. Incorporating complete switching from cigarettes to ENDS, the overall cessation rate was ~1.4x higher under Complete Authorization. Although Complete Prohibition was estimated to halve smoking initiation, the relative rate of ENDS to smoking initiation was ~3x higher under Complete Authorization. Together, greater ENDS availability and use under Complete Authorization produced lower smoking rates than Complete Prohibition, yielding 1.6 million fewer adult smoking- and vaping-attributable deaths (SVADs) and 28.9 million life-years gained (LYG). Sensitivity analyses indicated Complete Authorization could avert 0.01–4.3 million deaths, translating to 1.0–80.1 million LYG from 2024–2060. Conclusions Simulations consistently projected greater public health benefits from maintaining access to flavored ENDS via Complete Authorization. Flavored ENDS are preferred by most switchers and could accelerate cessation and reduce mortality, especially given enforcement challenges and illicit market responses. Public health impacts ultimately depend on the risk reduction of noncombustibles versus smoking among adults and the risk of initiation among otherwise never-users.

    Issues and Challenges to Education Policies and Practices in Nepal: A Descriptive and Factor Analysis [version 2; peer review: 1 approved, 1 approved with reservations]

    Background Despite many improvements in Nepal’s school education policies, various challenges persist at the implementation level. Policy evaluation has not sufficiently utilized the involvement of students, who are the primary stakeholders in education. Objective To identify the problems and challenges in Nepal’s education policies and practices from students’ perspectives. Methods Descriptive analysis (mean, SD) and principal component analysis (PCA) were conducted on the responses of 390 students (217 girls, 173 boys) from 73 community secondary schools in 7 provinces to a 15-question (5-point Likert) questionnaire. Results Satisfaction with overall educational quality was the lowest (Q4: 2.97), while regular examinations (Q10: 4.31) and class participation (Q9: 4.11) were the highest. PCA extracted three components (61.6% variance): PC1 Systemic Communication (34.1%), PC2 Digital Gap (16.3%), PC3 Quality-Drainage (11.2%). In particular, there was a 0.86 gap in Digital Tool Interest (Q6: 4.09) but no significant difference in Teacher Use (Q7: 3.23). The negative correlation of -0.35 between PC1 and PC3 indicated that schools with good communication were less likely to experience dropout. The -0.48 loading of Q3 confirmed that local school values ​​reduced dropout. Conclusion Three challenges — strengthening communication, digital training, and quality improvement — were identified. To address these challenges, local governments should urgently implement mandatory parent meetings, digital parent portals, teacher digital training, and rural school quality enhancement programs. Contribution/Originality This study makes a significant contribution by identifying the negative correlation between systemic communication and student dropout rates. This finding suggests that enhancing communication among local government, school management, and parents could effectively reduce dropout rates. Moreover, in providing empirical support for expectancy-value theory, this study highlights that the perception of value toward local schools is a crucial factor in student retention.

    Learning Trajectories Leave Decodable Traces in Neural Network Weight Space and Internal Representations

    arXiv:2611.24782v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Neural networks trained on the same task develop related weight-space structures, yet remain distinguishable after learning. Do these differences merely reflect alternative solutions, or do they preserve information about individual learning histories? We tested whether final weights reveal initialization, task order, and earlier trajectory states. Training reorganized the ensemble: initial pairwise distances did not consistently predict final distances. Nevertheless, all 30 trained networks were correctly matched to their initial weights among ten candidates per dataset. Networks sharing an initialization also developed recognizable final hidden representations across different batch schedules, although direct comparisons with initial activity did not recover identity. Learning order remained readable as well. A linear probe decoded the order of two earlier tasks with 100% accuracy on 80 held-out networks after ten epochs of a common final task. Earlier intermediate weight states were partly predictable, but individual initial weights could not be reliably reconstructed. Final weights thus retain selective historical information rather than a complete trajectory record. Perturbed networks recovered classification performance without returning to their unperturbed training paths. This separation persisted across datasets, intervention times, and network layers. Moreover, 2,157 of 2,160 aligned endpoint pairs showed no positive loss barrier at sampled interpolation points, indicating low-loss connections between distinct solutions rather than convergence to one weight vector. Together, these findings show that a shared task constrains networks to related successful solutions without erasing their individual histories. Final weights reflect not only what a network has learned, but also where learning began and how it unfolded.

    Hebbian self-organization of monosynaptic reflexes in a biologically realistic multiarticular limb

    arXiv:2611.23441v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Spinal monosynaptic reflexes depend on patterns of connectivity between muscle spindle afferents and motoneurons that should reflect musculoskeletal dynamics. One possibility is that the details of these circuits are specified genetically, but this requires an unlikely coevolution of musculoskeletal and neural elements. Here we show how biomechanical structure can determine the connectivity of self-organized spinal circuits. Using a neuromechanical model combining a multi-joint limb, proprioceptive feedback, and Hebbian plasticity driven by motor babbling, we find that biologically realistic connectivity emerges spontaneously when babbling is neither too sparse nor too frequent. This results in strong homonymous projections, weaker heteronymous projections among functional synergists, and very weak connections among antagonists. Overall, this study provides a mechanistic basis for fetal sensorimotor development and generates testable predictions about the consequences of naturally occurring and experimentally induced variations.

    ABSTRACT

    Meissner's corpuscles (MCs) are abundant in the glabrous skin of most primates, and are specialized to detect mechanical stimuli important in fine dynamic touch and precision grip. While MCs are known to be concentrated in the distal regions of the hands in both humans and nonhuman primates (NHPs), past work indicates that their size and density can vary substantially.
